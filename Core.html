    WORLD LINE ARCHIVE

    World lines are not parallel universes — they are revisions of the same universe. Every D-mail nudges history onto a different strand, and each strand has a will of its own: an attractor field that pulls outcomes back toward convergence, no matter who resists.

    ALPHA ATTRACTOR FIELD

    Every D-mail pulls the past further into SERN's hands. Here the lab is hunted, the year 2000 belongs to the Committee of 300, and Mayuri's death is written into the field itself — it repeats no matter how many times Okabe leaps. A world with no exit, only detours.

    BETA ATTRACTOR FIELD

    Undo the first D-mail and the tragedy simply changes shape: Kurisu dies on July 28th, and in 2036 the Third World War begins. The line where saving one person and saving seven billion are, at first, the same impossible choice.

    STEINS GATE — CONVERGENCE

    Neither Alpha nor Beta. A fabricated world line, reached by deceiving the past itself — one lie carried across time, and one paper that must survive a fire. No dystopia, no war, no deaths. This is the choice of Steins Gate.
